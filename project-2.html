What is matter?

matter is anything that has mass and takes up space.

There are 3 types of Matter.they are=

1 solid

2 liquid

3.gas

solid has a definite shape and volume.

liquid has a definite volume but no definite shape.

gas has no definite shape or volume.

examples for each state:

1. solid: ice, rock

2. liquid: water, oil

3. gas: air, steam

Note:water's scientific name is H₂O.

water is one of the most important substances on Earth.
